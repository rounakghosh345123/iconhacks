01 // MISSION BRIEF

More than a coding sprint.

IconHacks 2027 is designed to bring together ambitious student builders for a focused 24 hour hackathon. Teams will work on meaningful technical challenges, experiment with new ideas, receive guidance from mentors, and present working solutions to a panel of judges.

Organized by the IEEE Technology & Engineering Management Society and IEEE Computer Society Student Branch Chapters at Asansol Engineering College, IconHacks aims to create an environment where engineering, collaboration, and practical problem solving come together.

08 // EVENT PROTOCOL

Build fairly. Respect the room.

These summaries do not replace the complete approved event rules, which will be published by the organizers.

All substantive project implementation must follow the official codingwindow rules. Teams must use the approved repository and submission process.

Previously completed projects must not be presented as newly built during the hackathon. The official rules will define which pre existing assets are allowed.

Standard libraries, frameworks, APIs, and other permitted tools may be used according to the final rules. Teams must acknowledge third party assets and resources.

The final rules will explain project ownership and any limited permissions granted to organizers for event reporting and promotion.

The final rules will explain the code-freeze deadline, repository requirements, demonstration expectations, and late submission policy.

Respectful behavior is required. Harassment and discrimination are prohibited. A clear process for reporting incidents will be provided by the organizers.

Venue boundaries, overnight arrangements, emergency contacts, and participant safety expectations will be shared by the organizers.
